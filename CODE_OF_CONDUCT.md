# Code of Conduct

English · [中文](#中文)

## Our pledge

We want participation in megaui to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation. We act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and empathetic towards other people
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the community

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without
  their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take
appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they deem inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful. Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit,
or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this
Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
channel run by the project) and when an individual is officially representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the
project maintainers privately at <contact@gitmono.com>, marking the report as a conduct
issue. When private vulnerability reporting is enabled, the repository **Security** tab is
also accepted. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly
and fairly, and the privacy and safety of the reporter will be respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers follow these steps, in increasing severity: a private written warning; a public
warning for a specific period; a temporary ban from interaction; and a permanent ban.
The response is proportionate to the behaviour.

## 中文

### 我们的承诺

我们希望每个人参与 megaui 时都不会遭到骚扰，无论年龄、体型、残障、种族、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育背景、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、宗教或性取向如何。我们的言行应当有助于建立开放、友好、多元、包容、健康的社区。

### 行为标准

有助于营造良好环境的行为：对他人保持尊重与同理心；给出并坦然接受建设性反馈；为错误负责并从中学习；关注对项目和社区最有利的事。

不可接受的行为：带有性意味的语言或图像、不受欢迎的性关注；捣乱、侮辱或贬损性评论、人身或政治攻击；公开或私下的骚扰；未经许可公布他人的私人信息（如住址、邮箱）；其他在专业场合中会被合理认为不当的行为。

### 维护者的责任

项目维护者负责阐明并执行这些标准，并对他们认为不当、具有威胁性、冒犯性或有害的行为采取适当且公正的纠正措施。维护者有权也有责任删除、编辑或拒绝不符合本准则的评论、提交、代码、Issue 和其他贡献，并在适当时说明处理理由。

### 适用范围

本准则适用于所有项目空间（Issue、Pull Request、讨论以及项目运营的任何渠道），也适用于个人在公开场合正式代表项目时。

### 举报

遇到辱骂、骚扰或其他不可接受的行为，请发送到 <contact@gitmono.com> 私下举报，并注明这是行为准则问题。私密漏洞报告启用后，也可以使用仓库 **Security** 标签页。所有举报都会被及时、公正地审查和调查，并尊重举报者的隐私与安全。

### 处理方式

维护者按严重程度递增采取：私下书面警告；在一定期限内的公开警告；暂时禁止互动；永久禁止。处理与行为的严重程度相称。
